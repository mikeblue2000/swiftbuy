Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions? We're here to help.

How fast is SwiftBuy shipping?

Orders placed before 2 PM are processed same-day. Express delivery delivers within 24-48 hours across major cities, while standard delivery takes 2-3 business days.

What payment methods do you accept?

We support M-Pesa, Mobile Money, Visa, Mastercard, and Cash/Card on Delivery for eligible locations.

What is your return policy?

We offer a 30-day hassle-free return policy for all unused items in their original packaging.
